Privacy policy

Magnolia Games Studio apps store progress and settings on your device. Clear app data to delete them; device backups may retain copies.

Google Play processes purchases; apps use purchase records to provide access. AdMob processes device identifiers, IP addresses, interactions and diagnostics for ads, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google’s policy.

Analytics-enabled versions of Guess The Country, Mine a Mountain and Troll Hug Tower use Google Analytics to measure gameplay, purchases and ad revenue with app/device identifiers. Control Analytics and regional Privacy Choices in Settings. See Google’s use of app data.

If you agree to Advanced Analytics, these games send gameplay replay video, timestamped interaction history, purchase order records and random installation identifiers to private Firebase diagnostics storage to optimize the games and find bugs. Diagnostic recordings and logs are scheduled for deletion after seven days. Change your choice in Settings.

Optional online features share submitted information with their providers. You can skip optional features and rewarded ads. Manage advertising settings on your device.

This website adds no trackers. GitHub hosts it and processes request data. Providers retain data under their own policies.
